Partner FAQ

Common Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ is flexible and depends on the product and packaging. We support trial orders and mixed-category shipments so new partners can start small.

Can I become an exclusive distributor for my country?

Territory arrangements can be discussed with committed partners based on market potential and planned volumes. Share your business profile in the form below.

Do you provide samples?

Yes — samples can be arranged for qualified buyers before bulk orders. Our team will confirm the terms when you send your requirement.

Can products be supplied under our own brand?

Yes. Our OEM / private label service covers product selection or development, sampling, documentation, production and dispatch. See OEM services

Which documents do you provide for export?

Standard shipping documents plus product documents such as Certificate of Analysis, specifications and certificate copies. Market-specific requirements are confirmed per enquiry.
