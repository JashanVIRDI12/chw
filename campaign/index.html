    Buyer FAQ

    Straight answers.

    Can't find yours? — a person on our export desk replies.

    What is your minimum order quantity?

    MOQs depend on the product, pack and destination. Share your quantity in the enquiry and we will confirm the lowest workable MOQ — trial orders for new markets are welcome.

    Can I sell these products under my own brand?

    Yes. Our 85 nutraceutical formulations are available for private label, and we can discuss OEM packing for surgical products. Use the label studio above to send us a starting mockup.

    Do you provide samples?

    Yes — tick “Samples” in the WhatsApp enquiry and tell us which products you need. We confirm sample terms and courier details on chat.

    Which export documents do you support?

    We are a WHO-GMP certified manufacturer and support the standard export documentation importers need. Tell us your country and we will confirm the documents required for registration and clearance.

    Do you export Ayurvedic and herbal products?

    Yes. Our Vitantra range has 20 single-herb capsules — including Ashwagandha, Shilajit, Moringa, Turmeric and Triphala — made in our WHO-GMP facility. Tell us your country and we'll confirm registration and labelling requirements for herbal supplements there.

    Can I combine surgical, nutraceutical and herbal products in one shipment?

    Yes. All three ranges come from CHW, so you can consolidate them into a single order, invoice and shipment.

    How fast do you reply?

    Our export desk works on WhatsApp during Indian business hours (IST). Enquiries sent outside these hours are answered the next working day.
